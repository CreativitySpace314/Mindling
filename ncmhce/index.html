Visual clinical rules

EPISODE ≠ DISORDER
Single symptom cluster → identify the event. Recurrent pattern + duration + impairment → consider the disorder.
GOAL → OBJECTIVE → INTERVENTION
Goal = durable outcome. Objective = measurable client step. Intervention = what counselor/team does.
RISK → ACUITY → LEVEL OF CARE
Do not jump straight from scary content to hospitalization. Assess intent, plan, means, history, protective factors, and ability to maintain safety.
REFERRAL QUESTION → CONSTRUCT → TOOL
Choose an instrument because it measures the thing you need to know, not because its name sounds familiar.
FIRST / BEST / NEXT
Read the verb. “First” often means gather missing information or address immediate safety before treatment.
EMPATHY BEFORE FIXING
When no urgent safety issue exists, reflect and clarify before advice-giving, confrontation, or premature problem-solving.
